iPhone install guide

Put BookWare on your
Home Screen

Six steps, about a minute. Once it's installed it opens full screen with no address bar — just the library.

ℹ️
Before you start

You'll need Safari on your iPhone — Apple only allows Add to Home Screen from Safari, so Chrome and Firefox won't offer it. You'll also want your school Google account handy, since that's how you sign in.

Step-by-step instructions
1
Open Safari and go to BookWare
Open Safari on your iPhone and type in the BookWare address, or scan the QR code your teacher shared. You should land on the sign-in page with the Student and Teacher cards.
2
Tap the Share button
At the bottom of Safari, tap the Share button — a square with an arrow pointing up. If the toolbar isn't showing, scroll up slightly on the page to bring it back.
💡

It's the middle icon in the bottom bar — not the bookmark icon next to it.

3
Choose "Add to Home Screen"
A menu slides up from the bottom. Scroll down past the row of app icons until you see "Add to Home Screen" with a plus icon, and tap it. On newer iOS versions it may be tucked behind the three-dot "More" button.
💡

It sits below the row of app icons, usually about halfway down the list.

4
Name it and tap "Add"
A screen appears letting you name the icon. It should already say "BookWare" — leave it as-is or shorten it if you like. Then tap Add in the top-right corner.
5
Find the icon on your Home Screen
BookWare now sits on your Home Screen like any other app, with its own icon and the name you chose. Swipe up (or press the Home button) to go back and find it.
6
Open it and sign in
Tap the icon to launch BookWare. It opens full screen — no Safari address bar, no tabs. Pick Student or Teacher and sign in with your school Google account. It remembers you after that.
⚡

Signing in briefly hands you to Google's own page, so you'll see a bar with accounts.google.com in it for a moment. That's expected — it goes away once you're back.

🎉

You're all set!

BookWare is on your Home Screen. Open it to browse your teacher's shelves, check out books, and keep track of what you're reading.

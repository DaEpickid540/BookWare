Android install guide

Put BookWare on your
Home Screen

Six steps, about a minute. Once it's installed it opens full screen with no address bar — just the library.

ℹ️
Before you start

This works in any Android browser — Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or whatever you use. You'll also want your school Google account handy, since that's how you sign in.

Step-by-step instructions
1
Open your browser and go to BookWare
Open Chrome, Firefox, or your usual browser and type in the BookWare address, or scan the QR code your teacher shared. You should land on the sign-in page with the Student and Teacher cards.

At the top right of your browser, tap the three-dot menu (⋮). Look for "Install app", "Add to Home screen", or "Create shortcut".

The exact wording changes between browsers — look for anything mentioning install, home screen, or shortcut.

3
Select "Install app"
Tap the install option. A dialog appears asking you to confirm adding BookWare to your home screen.

Confirm and install
The app name should show as "BookWare" — tap Install or Add to put it on your home screen.

5
Find the icon on your Home Screen
BookWare now sits on your home screen like any other app. Press the home button to go back and find it — it may also appear in your app drawer.

6
Open it and sign in
Tap the icon to launch BookWare. It opens full screen — no address bar, no browser buttons. Pick Student or Teacher and sign in with your school Google account. It remembers you after that.

Signing in briefly hands you to Google's own page, so you'll see a bar with accounts.google.com in it for a moment. That's expected — it goes away once you're back.

🎉

You're all set!

BookWare is on your home screen. Open it to browse your teacher's shelves, check out books, and keep track of what you're reading.

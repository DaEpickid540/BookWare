What data BookWare stores

BookWare is a classroom library app for Mason City Schools. This page lists every piece of information it holds, exactly where that information lives, who can see it, and when it is deleted. Nothing is collected that is not on this page.

Last updated 2026-08-28 · applies to the BookWare app at bookware-site2.web.app

The short version

  • BookWare has no server of its own. There is no BookWare database, company, or backend. Data goes directly from your browser to Google Cloud Firestore, on Google's servers.
  • We never see or store a password. Sign-in is Google sign-in; Google handles the password and never shares it with BookWare.
  • Class rosters are deleted on the last day of school. Your teacher loses access to your name and email on that date automatically.
  • Book checkout records are deleted after 2 years.
  • Nothing is sold, advertised against, or shared with anyone outside your school, apart from the specific technical services listed at the bottom of this page.

The three places data can live

Google Cloud Firestore

The shared database, run by Google. This is the only place data is stored long-term and the only place other people (your teacher, an admin) can see it.

Google Account

Your name, email, and profile picture come from your Google account. Google holds these; BookWare reads them and copies the name and email into Firestore.

Your browser only

Settings saved in your own browser's storage. These never leave your device and BookWare cannot read them from anywhere else. Clearing site data erases them.

How deletion works

Two automatic rules run without anyone pressing a button:

On the last day of school

Every teacher has to set a last day of school for each class. On that date the class roster, meaning student names and emails, stops being readable by the teacher and is erased. The database enforces this cut-off itself rather than leaving it to the app, so it holds even if nobody opens BookWare that week.

After 2 years

A record of a book being borrowed and returned is deleted two years after the borrow date. Books still checked out are exempt: those are live records the library is using right now.

An honest limitation. BookWare runs entirely in the browser and has no scheduled server job. The access cut-off above is enforced by the database on every request and is exact. The erasure step is different: it runs when an administrator next opens the admin portal, so records can sit in an inaccessible-but-not-yet-deleted state for a while past their deadline. The admin portal lists every roster caught in that gap. Guaranteed same-day erasure would need a scheduled server job, which this app does not have.

Getting your data removed

  • Leave a library yourself. Settings → your libraries → Remove. This deletes your roster entry, with your name and email, from that teacher immediately.
  • Full account deletion. Ask an administrator. This deletes your account, removes you from every class roster, and strips your name from every checkout record in every library.
  • See what's held about you. Ask an administrator; the admin portal can export the whole record for you.

Districts normally require a signed data-privacy agreement with any vendor that receives student data. Switching ARIA on for student accounts is a decision for whoever handles those agreements at the district; it is not a settings toggle.
